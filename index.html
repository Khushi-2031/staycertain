About the risk score: a statistical model built from 85,370 past bookings at two hotels in Portugal (2015–2017). It puts a cancelled booking above a kept one about 73% of the time, and has not yet been tested on Indian hotels. No AI decides the score. Advance levels and savings figures rest on assumptions you can change in Settings. The sample hotel is fictional.
