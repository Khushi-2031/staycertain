StayCertain

Enter one booking to see how likely it is to be cancelled and what to do about it.

About this tool: trained on 87,216 bookings from two hotels in Portugal (2015–2017). It has not been tested on Indian hotels. It correctly ranks a cancelled booking above a kept one about 80% of the time, and at the Medium threshold it catches about 7 in 10 cancellations. Costs behind the thresholds are assumptions. Use it as a guide alongside your own judgement.
