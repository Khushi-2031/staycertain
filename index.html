About the score: a Logistic Regression model trained in Orange on 87,216 bookings from two hotels in Portugal (2015–2017); it ranks a cancelled booking above a kept one about 80% of the time and has not yet been tested on Indian hotels. The probability comes only from that formula — no AI decides the score. Deposit levels, reconfirmation rules and savings figures are business assumptions you can change in Settings. Sample data is fictional.
