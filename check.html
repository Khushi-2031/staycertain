Check a booking

Enter the booking as it was made. This shows the risk at the time of booking; the dashboard also updates it for the days left before arrival.

Result

Fill in the booking and press Check cancellation risk. The risk and the action to take will appear here.

How to read the result

  • LowBelow 30%. No action needed.
  • Medium30–54%. Send a WhatsApp reconfirmation 7 days before arrival.
  • High55% and above. Ask for a 50% advance and send a WhatsApp reconfirmation; call if there is no reply.

About this tool: built from 85,370 past bookings at two hotels in Portugal (2015–2017); not yet tested on Indian hotels. It puts a cancelled booking above a kept one about 73% of the time, and from the Medium level it flags about 7 in 10 cancellations. The risk levels come from cost assumptions. Use it as a guide alongside your own judgement.
